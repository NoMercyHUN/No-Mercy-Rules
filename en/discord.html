Community Rules (Discord & Ingame)

1. Respect

Treat all players and staff members with respect. Insults, harassment, and personal attacks are not permitted.

2. No Discrimination

Any racist, sexist, homophobic, religious, or other hateful behavior is strictly prohibited.

3. Politics and Religion

Political and religious debates, propaganda, or provocative content are not allowed on any server platform.

4. Threats and Harassment

Any real-life threats, intimidation, or harassment are strictly prohibited.

5. Community Conduct

Excessive toxicity, provocation, flaming, or intentionally harming the community atmosphere is not acceptable.

6. Voice Channel Usage

Loud music, abuse of voice changers, excessive noise, or intentionally disturbing other players is prohibited.

7. Respect for Staff

Decisions made by Admins and Moderators must be respected. Any disputes must be handled exclusively through tickets.

8. Common Sense Rule

Any behavior may be punishable if it is clearly against the interests of the community or the server, even if it is not specifically listed in these rules.

9. Rant Channel

A dedicated Rant channel is available on the Discord server.

Players may express their opinions more freely and engage in more casual discussions within the Rant channel.

Almost all topics are allowed in the Rant channel, except:

  • Racist remarks
  • Hate speech
  • Sharing or leaking personal information
  • Doxxing
  • Real-life threats
  • Sharing illegal content

The Admin Team reserves the right to take any necessary action when required.

Violating these rules may result in a warning, mute, kick, or permanent ban depending on the severity of the offense.
