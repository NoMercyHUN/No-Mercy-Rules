Discord Rules

Using the Discord server means you automatically accept these rules.

1. Respect

  • Respect every member of the community.
  • Harassment, threats, and intimidation are prohibited.
  • Racism, hate speech, and discrimination are prohibited.
  • Intentionally provoking other players is prohibited.

2. Communication

  • Civil and respectful communication is required.
  • Spam, flooding, and intentionally disrupting channels are prohibited.
  • Excessive swearing and aggressive communication are prohibited.
  • All channels must be used for their intended purpose.

3. Advertising

  • Advertising other DayZ servers is prohibited.
  • Sharing invites to other Discord servers is prohibited.
  • Advertising personal content or services without Admin approval is prohibited.

4. Usernames and Profiles

  • Impersonating an Admin or Staff member is prohibited.
  • Misleading usernames are prohibited.
  • Offensive or provocative profile pictures are prohibited.

5. Ticket System

  • Support-related matters must be handled through tickets.
  • Respectful communication is required in tickets.
  • Submitting fake or manipulated evidence is prohibited.
  • Opening multiple tickets for the same issue is prohibited.

6. Voice Chat Rules

  • The use of voice changers or disruptive audio software is prohibited.
  • Constantly talking over others is prohibited.
  • Playing music or noise through your microphone is prohibited.
  • Respectful behavior is required in voice channels as well.

7. Personal Information

  • Sharing another person's personal information is prohibited.
  • Doxxing is prohibited.
  • Threatening players using their real-life information is prohibited.

8. Cheat Accusations

  • Cheat accusations may only be submitted through a ticket.
  • Publicly calling another player a cheater is prohibited.
  • Making accusations without evidence is prohibited.

9. Media and Content

  • Sharing illegal or offensive content is prohibited.
  • Sharing NSFW content is prohibited.
  • Distributing viruses, scams, phishing attempts, or malicious links is prohibited.

10. Administration

  • Admin decisions are final.
  • Admins may make decisions based on the spirit of the rules.
  • Rule violations may result in warnings, mutes, kicks, or bans.
