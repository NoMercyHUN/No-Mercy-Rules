Discord Rules

Using the Discord server means you accept these rules.

1. Respect

  • Treat all community members with respect.
  • Harassment, threats, and intimidation are strictly prohibited.
  • Racism, hate speech, and discrimination are not tolerated.
  • Provoking or intentionally starting conflicts is forbidden.

2. Communication

  • Civil and respectful communication is required at all times.
  • Spam, flooding, and intentionally disrupting channels are prohibited.
  • Excessive swearing and aggressive behavior are not allowed.
  • Use channels for their intended purpose.

3. Advertising

  • Advertising other DayZ servers is prohibited.
  • Sharing invitations to other Discord servers is prohibited.
  • Advertising personal content, communities, or services requires Admin approval.

4. Usernames and Profiles

  • Impersonating Admins, Staff members, or other players is forbidden.
  • Misleading usernames are not allowed.
  • Offensive or inappropriate profile pictures are prohibited.

5. Ticket System

  • Support-related issues must be handled through tickets.
  • Respectful communication is required in tickets.
  • Submitting fake or manipulated evidence is prohibited.
  • Opening multiple tickets regarding the same issue is not allowed.

6. Voice Channels

  • Voice changers and disruptive audio software are prohibited.
  • Talking over others continuously is not allowed.
  • Playing music, soundboards, or loud noises without permission is prohibited.
  • The same behavior rules apply in voice channels as in text channels.

7. Personal Information

  • Sharing another person's personal information is strictly prohibited.
  • Doxxing is forbidden.
  • Threatening players using real-life information is prohibited.

8. Cheat Accusations

  • Cheat reports must be submitted through tickets.
  • Publicly accusing players of cheating is prohibited.
  • Accusations without evidence are not allowed.

9. Media and Content

  • Illegal, offensive, or harmful content may not be shared.
  • NSFW content is prohibited.
  • Viruses, scams, phishing attempts, and malicious links are strictly forbidden.

10. Administration

  • Admin decisions are final.
  • Admins may act based on the spirit of the rules, even if a situation is not explicitly listed.
  • Rule violations may result in warnings, mutes, kicks, temporary bans, or permanent bans.
