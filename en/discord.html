Discord Rules

Channel organization, respectful communication, and efficient support.

General

All general server rules also apply on Discord.

Channels

  • Use the appropriate channel for your messages.
  • Off-topic content should be posted in the designated off-topic channel.
  • Spam, flooding, and meaningless message chains are prohibited.
  • Intentionally disrupting channels or misusing them is not allowed.

Communication

  • Keep communication appropriate to the environment.
  • Provocation and intentionally escalating conflicts are prohibited.
  • Harassment or threats via private messages are also considered rule violations.

Advertising and Links

  • Advertising other servers without Admin approval is prohibited.
  • Sharing suspicious links, scams, or phishing attempts will result in an immediate ban.

Meta / Information Sharing

  • Sharing in-game information via Discord that provides an advantage is strictly prohibited.
  • This includes player locations, raid status, or event-related information.

Rant Channel

  • There is a dedicated “rant” channel where players may express their opinions more freely.
  • Raw, emotional communication and criticism of other players are allowed in this channel.
  • Admins are not obligated to intervene in discussions within this channel.
  • Use of this channel is at your own risk.

The following are still prohibited even in the rant channel:

  • Racism
  • Real-life threats or intimidation
  • Sharing personal information (doxxing)

Tickets

  • Ticket spam and troll tickets are prohibited.
  • Providing false information or attempting to mislead staff is not allowed.

Guidelines

  • Not knowing the rules does not exempt you from consequences.
  • All Admin decisions are final.
  • Admins reserve the right to take action in any situation that disrupts the community.
