General Rules

These rules apply to all players on both the server and Discord. By playing on the server or using our Discord, you automatically accept these rules.

Core Principle

The purpose of these rules is to ensure fair, enjoyable, and competitive gameplay.

The spirit of the rules always takes priority over literal interpretation.

A behavior may still be considered a rule violation even if it is not explicitly listed, if it provides an unfair advantage or attempts to bypass intended gameplay.

The Admin team reserves the right to treat such behavior as a rule violation.

Respect

  • Respect other players and all Admin team members.
  • Insults, racism, discrimination, and hate speech are prohibited.
  • Harassment, threats, and intimidation are prohibited.
  • Provoking or intentionally creating conflicts is prohibited.

Communication

  • Civil communication is required at all times.
  • Spam, flooding, and intentional disruption are prohibited.
  • Excessive swearing and aggressive behavior are prohibited.

Privacy & Personal Information

  • Sharing another person's personal information is prohibited.
  • Doxxing, blackmail, and targeted threats are strictly forbidden.

Advertising & Content

  • Advertising or self-promotion requires Admin approval.
  • Scams, phishing attempts, and malicious links are prohibited.
  • Sharing illegal content is prohibited.

Group Limits

  • A group may contain a maximum of 9 players.
  • No more than 6 members may be online at the same time.
  • Up to 3 players may remain in a reserve (sub-group) position.
  • Sub-group members may not participate in gameplay until they officially replace an active member.

Group Definition

A group is defined as any cooperation involving shared movement, shared PvP, shared raiding, shared bases, shared vehicles, or shared loot.

Any unofficial alliance between groups is prohibited.

Meta Gaming & Information Sharing

Using or sharing any out-of-game information that provides an advantage is prohibited.

  • Sharing player locations.
  • Sharing raid information.
  • Using information obtained from streams, Discord, or external sources.
  • Passing information between groups or players who are not actively involved.

Cheating & Exploits

The use of cheats, hacks, scripts, bugs, exploits, or unintended mechanics is strictly prohibited.

  • Cheats, hacks, scripts, or third-party software.
  • Duplication (Duping).
  • Moving items, players, or vehicles through walls, doors, or other objects.
  • Vortexing.
  • Base glitching.
  • Collision bug abuse.
  • Moonwalk abuse.
  • Animation abuse.
  • Any method that bypasses normal gameplay mechanics.

Exploit abuse is considered a severe rule violation and may result in immediate punishment.

Reporting Rule Violations

  • Reports must be submitted through a ticket.
  • False reports may result in punishment.
  • Evidence may be required during investigations.

Punishments

Any rule violation may result in punishment.

The severity of the punishment is determined by the Admin team.

Guidelines

  • Not knowing the rules is not an excuse.
  • The rules may be updated at any time.
  • Open a ticket for disputes instead of arguing in chat.
  • Admin decisions are final.
