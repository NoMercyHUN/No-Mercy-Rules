General Rules

These community rules apply at all times — both on Discord and on the server.

Core Principle

The purpose of these rules is to ensure fair, enjoyable, and competitive gameplay for everyone.

The spirit of the rules always takes precedence over literal interpretation.

Any behavior that provides an unfair advantage or attempts to bypass intended gameplay may be treated as a rule violation, even if not explicitly listed.

Admins reserve the right to enforce this at their discretion.

Respect

  • Treat all players and Admins with respect.
  • Insults, racism, and hate speech are strictly prohibited.
  • Harassment, threats, and intimidation are not tolerated.
  • Provoking or intentionally escalating conflicts is prohibited.

Communication

  • Maintain a respectful and appropriate tone at all times.
  • Spam, flooding, and intentional disruption are not allowed.
  • Excessive swearing or aggressive behavior is prohibited.

Privacy

  • Sharing personal information is strictly prohibited.
  • Doxxing, blackmail, or targeted threats will result in immediate action.

Advertising and Content

  • Advertising or self-promotion without Admin approval is not allowed.
  • Scams, phishing attempts, or malicious links are strictly prohibited.
  • Sharing illegal content is forbidden.

Group Rules

  • A group may consist of a maximum of 9 players.
  • No more than 6 players may be active (online) at the same time.
  • Up to 3 players may be kept as a sub group (offline reserve).
  • Sub group members may not participate in gameplay until they join the active 6-player roster.

Group Definition

A group is defined as any coordinated cooperation, including shared movement, PvP, raiding, or base usage — regardless of whether players are officially grouped.

Any form of unofficial cooperation (alliances) is strictly prohibited.

Meta / Information Sharing

Using or sharing out-of-game information that provides any kind of advantage is strictly prohibited.

  • Sharing player locations.
  • Sharing raid-related information.
  • Using information obtained from streams or external sources.

Exploits and Glitches

Abuse of bugs, glitches, or exploits is strictly prohibited.

  • Moving items, players, or vehicles through walls, doors, or other objects is not allowed.
  • So-called "vortexing" is prohibited.
  • Item duplication is strictly forbidden.
  • Glitching into bases is prohibited.
  • Exploiting collision bugs is not allowed.

Any method that deviates from intended game mechanics is considered a violation.

Exploiting will be treated as a severe offense.

Punishments

Any rule violation will result in disciplinary action.

The severity of the punishment will be determined by the Admin team.

Guidelines

  • Not knowing the rules does not exempt you from consequences.
  • The rules may be updated at any time.
  • In case of disputes, open a ticket — do not argue in chat.
  • All Admin decisions are final.
