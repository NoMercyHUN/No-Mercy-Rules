Base Building Rules

All bases must remain fairly raidable at all times.

1. Flag Usage

  • Only 1 flag may be placed per group.
  • Each group may only have 1 base.
  • If you are a member of a flag territory, that base counts as your base.
  • You may not place additional flags.
  • You may not use other bases for storing gear or loot.
  • Only one existing vanilla building may be built into.

2. Base Location

It is forbidden to build a base in or on the following locations/buildings:

  • Fire stations.
  • Medical buildings, such as hospitals or clinics.
  • Castles.
  • Police stations.
  • Military buildings or areas that spawn military loot.

Your base must not have direct line of sight to the following locations:

  • Military areas.
  • In-game events, except Airdrops.
  • Keycard rooms.
  • Traders.
  • Bunker.

Watchtowers are allowed, but any line of sight towards these areas must be blocked or covered. You may not watch or shoot these locations from your base.

3. Bases Built on Water

Bases may be built on lakes, rivers, or other bodies of water, but they must have an entrance that is reachable and raidable on foot from land.

4. Road Usage

Your base must not directly touch a road, and no objects may be placed on the roadway.

Exception: Bases built at the end of dead-end roads, where vehicles would not be able to continue beyond the base anyway.

5. Structural Requirements

  • The maximum base size is 5x5x5 normal BBP elements.
  • This means 5 elements wide, 5 elements long, and 5 elements high.
  • No part of the base may extend outside the 5x5x5 area.
  • Any element sticking out of this area may be deleted.
  • If angled walls are used on the upper level, the maximum allowed overhang is half a BBP element.
  • The base must always be connected to the ground and must have proper support.
  • For bases built on water, support does not need to be placed under floors below the water surface.
  • A base built on an existing vanilla building may only be 2 levels higher than the original roof of the building.
  • Please note that the flag only protects 8 meters downward.
  • Floating bases are forbidden. A maximum of 1 foundation element may be unsupported.

6. Traps and Inaccessible Areas

  • It is forbidden to build trap areas where raiders can only escape by dying.
  • The base must never be unraidable at any time.
  • Every room and every stored item inside the base must be reachable on foot through doors.
  • This rule also applies to vehicles.
  • You may not hide loot or vehicles in places that cannot be reached during a normal raid.

7. Doors and Codelocks

  • Stacking doors is forbidden.
  • There must be at least half a BBP floor distance between doors.
  • Exception: Existing vanilla buildings where the original doorways are naturally closer together.
  • A maximum of 15 codelocked doors may be built per base.

8. Dismantling and Rebuilding

It is forbidden to dismantle and rebuild parts of your base after each use in order to make an area accessible only to you.

9. Shortcuts

Windows may be used as shortcuts inside the base, but if attackers destroy all doors, they must be able to access 100% of the base.

  • Airlocks or rooms that can only be accessed through windows are forbidden.
  • Every window shortcut must be passable.

10. Use of Other Items

  • It is forbidden to reinforce or block doors, gates, or base sections with any items.
  • It is forbidden to block base entrances with immovable objects or vehicles.
  • Barbed wire may not be used in narrow corridors.
  • Forcing players into barbed wire is forbidden.

11. One-Sided Advantages

Defensive base elements must not provide an unfair one-sided advantage to defenders.

The following building methods are forbidden:

  • Toe peek.
  • Grenade holes.
  • Death tunnels.
  • Abusing forced animations.
  • Pixel peek, foot peek, gap shooting, or any hitbox/model exploit.
  • Building a base that requires multiple players, boosting, helicopters, or extra tools to raid.

Minimum allowed corridor width: half a BBP wall width, for example a half door kit.

If you are unsure about any building rule, open a ticket and ask the Admin team.

The Admin team reserves the right to classify, modify, or remove any base that provides an unfair advantage or violates the spirit of the rules.

12. Vehicle Storage

  • A maximum of 4 cars and 3 large helicopters may be stored inside one base.
  • A maximum of 5 gyrocopters may be stored.
  • Additional vehicles must be locked but stored outside the base so other players can attempt to steal them.
  • Vehicles stored inside the base must be stealable.
  • It is forbidden to build around, under, or above vehicles to prevent them from being stolen during a raid.

13. Tents and Storage

  • Tents and storage containers may not be placed inside each other.
  • Each tent must be placed separately on the ground or on a floor element.
  • Stacking tents or storage containers is forbidden.

14. Logging Out Inside Another Player's Base

It is forbidden to log out inside another player's base, except during a server restart.

If you are logged out inside another player's base due to a server restart, you must reconnect within 5 minutes after the server comes back online.

Breaking this rule may be treated as rule abuse.

15. If a Base Is Built Around You

If you log in and find that a base has been built around you while you were offline:

  • You must open a ticket with the Admin team.
  • You may not take any loot or items from that base.
  • You may not use the situation to your advantage.
