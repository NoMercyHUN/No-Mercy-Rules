Building Rules

Building is allowed as long as bases remain fair, fully raidable, and do not negatively impact gameplay.

Core Principle

All bases must be fully raidable in a fair manner and must not rely on unintended mechanics or exploit any game flaws.

Even if a base is technically compliant, it may be deemed illegal if it provides an unfair advantage or is designed to bypass raid mechanics.

1. General Rules

  • Each group may have a maximum of 1 flagpole.
  • A maximum of 4 cars and 5 helicopters may be stored inside a base.
  • Any additional vehicles must be stored outside of the base perimeter.

2. Placement Restrictions

  • Building is prohibited near military zones or event locations, or in any way that provides direct line of sight to them.
  • Building on roads or any vehicle-accessible routes is not allowed.
  • Building in police stations, prisons, hospitals, or medical facilities is prohibited.

3. Structural Requirements

  • The base must be connected to the ground or to an existing structure.
  • Floating bases are not allowed. A maximum of 1 foundation piece may extend beyond the edge of the base.
  • The maximum base size is limited to 5x5 foundations with a maximum height of 4 levels.
  • The base must be raid-accessible by a single player on foot without requiring boosting, glitching, or dismantling exploits.

5. Prohibited Building Methods

  • Tent stacking (placing smaller tents inside larger tents) is prohibited.
  • Trap bases are not allowed.
  • Using barbed wire inside the base is prohibited.
  • Any layout that forces specific player animations (jumping, crouching, prone, etc.) is prohibited.
  • Any structure designed around collision or hitbox exploitation is strictly prohibited.
  • Any building technique that forces players to walk across thin beams, narrow objects, or similar surfaces during a raid is prohibited.
  • This specifically includes using floor pieces as gates or barriers in a way that artificially blocks or restricts normal raid paths.

5. Prohibited Building Methods

  • Tent stacking is not allowed (placing smaller tents inside larger ones).
  • Trap bases are prohibited.
  • Barbed wire may not be used inside the base.
  • Any setup that forces specific player animations (jumping, crouching, prone, weapon raising, etc.) is prohibited.
  • Structures designed to exploit collision or hitbox mechanics are not allowed.

6. Raid Fairness Requirements

  • Bases must not contain unraidable areas or inaccessible loot.
  • Tunnels and raid paths must be at least two small-wall widths wide.
  • "Fake raidable" bases (appearing raidable but actually not) are prohibited.

7. Peek Rules

  • Shooting and visibility are only allowed through BBP (BaseBuildingPlus) windows.
  • All other forms of peeking are prohibited.
  • Pixel peeking, foot peeking, gap shooting, or any hitbox/model abuse is strictly prohibited.
  • Any such exploit will be treated as a serious rule violation.

Admin Authority

Admins reserve the right to declare any base illegal, modify it, or remove it if it provides an unfair advantage or violates the spirit of the rules.
