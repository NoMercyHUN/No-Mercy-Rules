Base Building Rules

1. Flag Usage

1.1. Only 1 flag may be placed per group = Each team may have only 1 base.

1.2. If you are a member of a flag area, that base counts as your base.

1.3. You may not place additional flags.

1.4. You may not use other bases to store equipment or loot.

1.5. Only one existing (vanilla) building may be built into.

2. Location

2.1. Building a base is prohibited in the following locations or buildings:

  • Fire stations
  • Medical buildings (hospitals, clinics, etc.)
  • Castles
  • Police stations
  • Military buildings or areas that spawn military loot

2.2. A base may not have direct line of sight to the following locations:

  • Military areas
  • In-game events (except Air Drops)
  • Keycard rooms
  • Traders
  • Bunker

Watchtowers may be built; however, any line of sight toward these locations must be blocked or sealed off. These locations may not be observed or shot at from a base. Violation of this rule will result in an immediate ban.

2.3. Bases may be built on lakes, rivers, or water surfaces; however, they must have a raid-able entrance that can be reached on foot from land.

3. Road Usage

A base may not directly touch a road, and no object may be placed on the roadway. Exception: Bases built at the end of dead-end roads where vehicles could not continue past the base anyway.

4. Structural Requirements

4.1. A base may be a maximum size of 5x5x5 (BBP standard elements).

That means 5 elements wide, 5 elements long, and 5 elements high.

4.2. No part of the base may extend outside the 5x5x5 volume. Any element extending beyond this area may be removed.

If the upper level of the base uses angled walls to provide visibility along the base walls, an overhang of up to half a BBP element is permitted.

4.3. Bases must always be connected to the ground and properly supported. Exception: Bases built on water surfaces. In this case, support structures are not required underneath floors below the water.

4.4. A base built onto an existing (vanilla) building may extend a maximum of 2 floors above the original roof. (Keep in mind that the flag only protects 8 meters downward.)

4.5. Floating bases are prohibited. A maximum of 1 foundation element may exist without support.

4.6. It is prohibited to build trap sections from which raiders cannot escape unless they die.

4.7. Door stacking is prohibited. Doors must be placed at least half a BBP floor apart. Exception: Existing (vanilla) buildings where doors are already closer together. In such cases, BBP doors may be placed in the original doorway positions.

5. Codelocks

A maximum of 15 codelocked doors may be built per base.

6. Dismantling and Rebuilding

It is prohibited to repeatedly dismantle and rebuild parts of your base after each use in order to make areas accessible only to yourself.

7. Shortcuts

Windows may be used as shortcuts within a base; however, if attackers destroy all doors, they must be able to access 100% of the base. So-called "airlocks" or any room accessible only through windows are prohibited. All window shortcuts must be fully passable.

8. Bases Must Always Be Raid-able

8.1. Every room and every stored item within a base must be reachable on foot through a door.

This rule also applies to vehicles.

8.2. You may not hide loot or vehicles in places that cannot be reached during a normal raid.

Punishment: Illegal structures or items may be removed without warning, and involved players may receive a 48-hour ban.

9. Use of Other Items

9.1. It is prohibited to reinforce or block doors, gates, or sections of a base with objects in a way that makes them inaccessible during a raid or requires methods other than standard raiding tools (C4).

9.2. Intentionally blocking base entrances with immovable objects or vehicles is prohibited.

9.3. Using barbed wire in narrow hallways is prohibited. Intentionally forcing players into barbed wire is also prohibited.

10. Unfair Defensive Advantages

Defensive structures inside a base may not provide an unfair one-sided advantage to defenders.

The following building techniques are prohibited:

Toe Peek – Any design that allows defenders to shoot attackers while attackers cannot effectively shoot back.

Grenade Hole – Any opening, gap, or passage that allows grenades to be thrown into an area where attackers cannot hit the player throwing them.

Death Tunnel – Narrow corridors or passages that force attackers to crouch or crawl, or otherwise prevent normal weapon use and aiming. The minimum permitted hallway width is half a BBP wall width (Half Door Kit).

Forced Animation Abuse – For example, traps built around ladders, windows, or firing ports that make a climbing player's movement predictable and easy to kill.

Requiring Multiple Players or Extra Tools – Every base must be raid-able by a single player. You may not build a base that requires boosting, helicopters, or tools other than standard raid tools (C4) to enter.

Pixel peeks, foot peeks, gap shooting, and any hitbox/model exploits are strictly prohibited.

If you are unsure about the interpretation of any building rule, contact the Admin Team through a ticket before building.

The Admin Team reserves the right to classify, modify, or remove any base deemed illegal, unfair, or against the spirit of the rules.
