Base Building Rules

All bases must remain fairly raidable at all times.

1. Flag Usage

  • Only 1 flag may be placed per group.
  • Each group may only have 1 base.
  • If you are a member of a flag territory, that base counts as your base.
  • You may not place additional flags.
  • You may not use other bases to store gear or loot.
  • Only one existing vanilla building may be incorporated into a base.

2. Base Location

Building a base in the following locations or buildings is prohibited:

  • Fire stations.
  • Medical buildings such as hospitals and clinics.
  • Castles.
  • Police stations.
  • Military buildings or areas that spawn military loot.

Bases may not have direct line of sight to the following locations:

  • Military areas.
  • In-game events, except Airdrops.
  • Keycard rooms.
  • Traders.
  • Bunkers.

Watchtowers are allowed, however any line of sight towards these areas must be blocked or covered. Players may not observe or shoot at these locations from their base.

3. Water Bases

Bases may be built on lakes, rivers, or other bodies of water, however they must have a raidable entrance that can be reached on foot from land.

4. Road Usage

A base may not directly touch a road, and no objects may be placed on the roadway.

Exception: Bases built at the end of dead-end roads where vehicles would not be able to continue past the base anyway.

5. Structural Requirements

  • The maximum base size is 5x5x5 standard BBP elements.
  • This means 5 elements wide, 5 elements long, and 5 elements high.
  • No part of the base may extend outside the 5x5x5 area.
  • Any element extending beyond this area may be removed.
  • When using angled walls, the maximum allowed overhang is half a BBP element.
  • The base must always be connected to the ground and properly supported.
  • Bases built on water do not require supports extending beneath the water under floor pieces.
  • A base built onto an existing vanilla building may be no more than 2 levels higher than the original roof.
  • Please note that a flag only protects 8 meters downward.
  • Floating bases are prohibited. A maximum of 1 foundation element may remain unsupported.

6. Traps and Inaccessible Areas

  • It is prohibited to build trap areas from which raiders cannot escape except by dying.
  • A base may never become completely unraidable.
  • Every room and every stored item must be reachable on foot through doors.
  • This rule also applies to vehicles.
  • You may not hide loot or vehicles in locations that cannot be reached during a normal raid.

7. Doors and Codelocks

  • Door stacking is prohibited.
  • A minimum distance of half a BBP floor must exist between doors.
  • Exception: Existing vanilla buildings where doors are naturally closer together.
  • A maximum of 15 codelocked doors may be built per base.

8. Dismantling and Rebuilding

It is prohibited to dismantle and rebuild parts of your base after every use in order to make an area accessible only to yourself.

9. Shortcuts

Windows may be used as shortcuts inside the base. However, if attackers destroy all doors, they must be able to access 100% of the base.

  • Airlocks or rooms that can only be accessed through windows are prohibited.
  • Every window shortcut must be passable.

10. Use of Other Items

  • Using any item to reinforce or block doors, gates, or base sections is prohibited.
  • Blocking base entrances with immovable objects or vehicles is prohibited.
  • Using barbed wire in narrow corridors is prohibited.
  • Intentionally forcing players into barbed wire is prohibited.

11. Unfair Defensive Advantages

Defensive structures inside a base may not provide an unfair one-sided advantage to defenders.

The following building methods are prohibited:

  • Toe peek.
  • Grenade hole.
  • Death tunnel.
  • Abusing forced animations.
  • Pixel peeks, foot peeks, gap shooting, and any hitbox or model exploit.
  • Building a base that requires multiple players, boosting, helicopters, or extra tools to raid.

Minimum allowed corridor width: half a BBP wall width, such as a Half Door Kit.

If you are unsure about any rule interpretation, open a ticket and contact the Admin Team.

The Admin Team reserves the right to classify any base as illegal, modify it, or remove it if it provides an unfair advantage or violates the spirit of the rules.

12. Vehicle Storage

  • A maximum of 4 cars and 3 large helicopters may be stored inside a base.
  • A maximum of 5 gyrocopters may be stored.
  • Additional vehicles must be locked but stored outside the base so other players have the opportunity to steal them.
  • Vehicles stored inside the base must remain stealable.
  • Building around, underneath, or above vehicles to prevent them from being stolen during a raid is prohibited.

13. Tents and Storage

  • Tents and storage containers may not be placed inside one another.
  • Each tent must be placed individually on the ground or on a floor piece.
  • Stacking tents or storage containers is prohibited.

14. Logging Out Inside Another Player's Base

Logging out inside another player's base is prohibited, except during a server restart.

If a server restart causes you to remain inside another player's base, you must reconnect within 5 minutes after the server comes back online.

Violating this rule may be considered rule abuse.

15. If a Base Is Built Around You

If a player logs in and discovers that a base has been built around them while they were offline:

  • They must open a ticket with the Admin Team.
  • They may not take any loot or items from that base.
  • They may not use the situation for personal advantage.
