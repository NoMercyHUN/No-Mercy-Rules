Punishments

The severity of a punishment depends on the rule violation and its circumstances.

Available Punishments

  • Warning
  • Mute
  • Kick
  • Temporary Ban
  • Permanent Ban
  • Character Wipe
  • Removal of Illegally Obtained Items
  • Vehicle Removal
  • Removal of Illegal Base Structures

Immediate Ban Offenses

  • Cheating, hacking, or script usage
  • Exploit abuse
  • Duplication (Duping)
  • Moonwalking, Animation Abuse, or other prohibited exploits
  • Doxxing or sharing personal information
  • Serious threats or harassment
  • Ban evasion using alternative accounts
  • Submitting fake evidence during an admin investigation

Raid & Base Rule Violations

  • Illegally obtained loot may be removed.
  • Illegal base structures may be deleted.
  • The offending player may receive a temporary or permanent ban.

Safe Zone Violations

  • Violating Safe Zone rules may result in warnings or bans.
  • The Admin team may remove vehicles or items involved in the violation.

Appeals

  • Appeals must be submitted through a support ticket.
  • Include your Steam64 ID.
  • Provide the date and time of the punishment.
  • Attach any available evidence.

General Information

  • Any rule violation may result in punishment.
  • The Admin team reserves the right to determine the appropriate punishment.
  • Repeated violations may result in more severe punishments.
  • Admin decisions are final.
  • Not knowing the rules is not an excuse.
