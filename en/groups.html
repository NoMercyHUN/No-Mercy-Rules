Group Rules

Rules regarding groups and cooperation between players.

Group Limit

  • A group may consist of a maximum of 9 players.
  • No more than 6 players may be online at the same time.
  • The remaining 3 players may only serve as reserve members (sub group).
  • Sub group members may not participate in any active gameplay until they officially replace a member of the active 6-player roster.

What Counts as a Group?

A group is defined as any group of players that:

  • Moves together.
  • Participates in PvP together.
  • Raids together.
  • Uses the same base.
  • Shares loot or vehicle storage.

It does not matter whether the players are officially in the same group or not.

Alliance Rules

  • Any permanent or recurring alliance is prohibited.
  • Two or more groups may not cooperate during PvP.
  • Two or more groups may not defend the same base together.
  • Two or more groups may not raid together.
  • Using shared communication channels for the purpose of gaining an advantage is prohibited.

Raid Assistance

  • Calling outside players or other groups for assistance during a raid is prohibited.
  • Sharing raid information with other groups is prohibited.
  • Joining an ongoing raid as a third party alliance is prohibited.

Group Switching

  • A player who leaves a group may only join another group with Admin approval.
  • A departing player may not take group information, loot, or vehicles with them.
  • Attempting to bypass group switching rules may result in punishment.

Metagaming

Using or sharing external information that provides an advantage to another group is prohibited.

  • Sharing raid locations.
  • Sharing base coordinates.
  • Sharing the positions of online players.
  • Using information obtained through Discord, streams, or other external sources.

Multiple Bases

  • A group may only own the number of bases permitted by the rules.
  • Creating alternate groups to store additional bases is prohibited.
  • Attempting to bypass this rule may result in Admin action.

Responsibility

Every member of a group is responsible for the actions of the group.

  • If a group member breaks a rule, the Admin Team may investigate the entire group.
  • Knowingly cooperating with cheaters is punishable in the same manner.
