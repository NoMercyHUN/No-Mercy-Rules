Group and Alliance Rules

1. A group may contain a maximum of 9 players. (6 active and 3 reserve players)

2. Out of these, a maximum of 6 players may be online at the same time.

3. It is possible to keep 3 players in a sub group (offline reserve) role.

4. Sub group members may not participate in gameplay until they join the 6-player active roster.

5. Alliances are not allowed. You are not required to be hostile toward every player, however:

  • you may not enter another group’s base for any purpose other than raiding,
  • you may not assist them during an attack or raid,
  • you may not move together around events (Drop, KOTH), keycard rooms (e.g. Tisy, NWAF, Krasno, etc.) or the bunker for the purpose of shared looting or hunting someone/some people.

If this rule is violated, the involved groups may be banned, and both bases may be deleted.

6. Regularly switching groups in order to bypass the rules is prohibited. If you are a member of a group, stay in it. In case of a genuine group change, opening a ticket is required.

7. If it is discovered that any member of a group uses or owns multiple bases or teams, the extra base(s) will be deleted without warning, and the involved players may be banned.

8. Trading is allowed, and the recommended location is the safe zone trader area. This must always be arranged through private messages.

Everyone trades at their own responsibility.

We are unable to compensate any losses that occur during trading.

9. During raid time, all trading and cooperation with anyone who is not part of your team is prohibited.

10. Admins do not get involved in internal group conflicts, theft, or ownership disputes.
