Group Rules

These rules regulate group size, cooperation, alliances, and group-related gameplay.

1. Group Limit

  • A group may consist of a maximum of 9 players.
  • No more than 6 members may be online at the same time.
  • Up to 3 players may remain in a reserve (sub-group) position.
  • Sub-group members may not participate in gameplay until they officially replace an active member.

2. What Counts as a Group?

A group is defined as players who:

  • Move together.
  • Participate in PvP together.
  • Raid together.
  • Share a base.
  • Share vehicles or storage.
  • Regularly cooperate for a common goal.

It does not matter whether the players are officially part of the same in-game group.

3. Alliances

  • Permanent or recurring alliances between groups are prohibited.
  • Two or more groups may not fight together during PvP.
  • Two or more groups may not defend the same base together.
  • Two or more groups may not raid together.
  • Sharing intelligence, locations, or raid information between groups is prohibited.

4. Third-Party Participation

  • Calling another group for assistance during PvP is prohibited.
  • Calling another group to help defend a base is prohibited.
  • Joining an ongoing raid to support either side is prohibited.
  • Third-party involvement may be treated as an alliance violation.

5. Group Switching

  • Players may not switch groups during an active raid.
  • Players may not switch groups during active PvP situations.
  • Group switching to bypass group limits is prohibited.
  • Using alternate groups to gain advantages is prohibited.

6. Meta Gaming Between Groups

Sharing information between separate groups is prohibited.

  • Sharing player locations.
  • Sharing base locations.
  • Sharing raid information.
  • Providing real-time callouts to another group.
  • Using Discord, streams, or external communication to support another group.

7. Alternate Groups

  • Creating secondary groups to bypass group limits is prohibited.
  • Creating storage groups for extra bases or loot is prohibited.
  • Creating groups for vehicle storage beyond normal limits is prohibited.

8. Shared Bases

  • A base may belong to only one group.
  • Multiple groups may not share a base.
  • Multiple groups may not share vehicle storage.
  • Multiple groups may not use the same flag territory.

9. Responsibility

Every member of a group is responsible for the actions of the group.

  • The Admin team may investigate an entire group if one member violates the rules.
  • Knowingly cooperating with cheaters is punishable.
  • Assisting players who are breaking the rules may result in punishment.

10. Admin Discretion

The Admin team reserves the right to investigate any suspicious cooperation between players.

If a situation clearly violates the spirit of the group rules, it may still be punished even if it is not explicitly listed above.
