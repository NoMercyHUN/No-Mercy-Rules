Raid Rules

All raids must be conducted fairly. Any attempt to bypass the raid system or gain an unintended advantage is prohibited.

1. Raid Definition

A raid is any attempt to enter another player's base with the intention of gaining access to loot or the base itself.

This includes both Soft Raids and Standard Raids.

2. Raid Recording

  • Raiders must record the entire raid.
  • The recording must begin before entry and continue until leaving the base.
  • Recordings may only be requested by the Admin Team.
  • Raid recordings will never be shared with other players.
  • Failure to provide a recording may result in a 3-day ban.
  • Raid recordings must be kept for at least 5 days.

3. Standard Raid

  • Breaching Charges may only be used on doors, hangar doors, hatches, garage doors, and gates.
  • Walls and windows may not be destroyed using explosives.
  • A raid becomes active when the first charge is placed or the first wall is dismantled.
  • A raid remains active for 60 minutes.
  • The 60-minute timer resets every time another gate or door is breached.

4. Raid Times

  • Friday: 18:00 - 24:00 (CEST)
  • Saturday: 16:00 - 24:00 (CEST)

5. Soft Raid Times

Soft Raiding is allowed 24/7.

6. Approved Raid Tools

  • Breaching Charges
  • Heavy Breaching Charges
  • Hacksaw / Raid Hacksaw
  • Hatchet

7. Rebuilding Rules

  • Only previously destroyed gates may be rebuilt during a raid.
  • New gates may not be constructed.
  • Defenders may rebuild destroyed codelocked gates if no damage has occurred for 60 minutes.
  • All attackers must have left the base area.
  • Tracking the timer is the defender's responsibility.

8. Defender Rules

  • Logging out with valuable loot during a raid is prohibited.
  • Admins may investigate suspicious logouts.
  • Characters may be killed and loot transferred if this rule is violated.
  • This rule also applies if the raid continues through a server restart.
  • Destroying or ruining your own loot during a raid is prohibited.
  • Dismantling storage to deny loot is prohibited.
  • Blocking entrances or forcing animations is prohibited.
  • Changing group members during an active raid is prohibited.

9. Attacker Rules

  • Logging out inside another player's base is prohibited.
  • Logging out with raid loot is prohibited.
  • Logging out to avoid consequences during an active raid is prohibited.
  • Placing codelocks on another player's base is prohibited.
  • Claiming another group's flag territory is prohibited.
  • Changing group members during an active raid is prohibited.
  • Destroying structures for resources when an entry route exists is prohibited.
  • Blocking doors or entrances is prohibited.
  • Destroying acquired loot is prohibited.
  • Only take what you need.
  • Loot that is not taken must be returned to containers.

10. Breaching Charge Guide

  • Tier 1: 2 Homemade Breaching Charges or 1 Heavy Breaching Charge.
  • Tier 2: 4 Homemade Breaching Charges or 2 Heavy Breaching Charges.
  • Tier 3: 6 Homemade Breaching Charges or 3 Heavy Breaching Charges.

11. Soft Raid & Boosting Rules

  • Boosting using one player or one vehicle is allowed.
  • Stacking items or multiple vehicles is prohibited.
  • The maximum boosting limit is one player or one vehicle.
  • Helicopter boosting is prohibited.
  • Using glitches or bugs to enter a base is prohibited.
  • Entering through vanilla windows without collision or using vanilla ladders as intended is not considered glitching.
  • All soft raids and boosting activities must be recorded.
  • Failure to provide a recording may result in punishment.

12. Helicopter Usage

  • Landing helicopters inside or on top of another player's base is prohibited.
  • If you land inside an enemy territory and exit the helicopter, the game may move you outside the zone.
  • The helicopter may remain inside the territory.
  • Any helicopter loss resulting from this mechanic will not be reimbursed.
