Raid Rules

These rules are designed to ensure fair, transparent, and verifiable raids.

Core Principle

All raids must be conducted in a fair manner. Any method that bypasses raid mechanics or relies on unintended advantages is strictly prohibited.

1. Raid Definition

A raid is defined as any attempt to breach a player's base for the purpose of gaining access or obtaining loot.

A raid officially begins with the use of a Breaching Charge.

2. Breaching Charge Usage

  • Breaching Charges may only be used on doors, hangar doors, garage doors, and gates.

3. Raid Times (C4 / Breaching Charge)

  • Friday 19:00–23:00 / Saturday 19:00–23:00 (CET)

4. Soft Raiding (Allowed 24/7)

Soft raiding refers to any method of entry that does not require a Breaching Charge.

  • Jumping or climbing into a base
  • Entering through windows or crawling in
  • "Soft siding" due to clear building mistakes
  • Using open or unsecured entrances

Any method involving glitches, boosting, or unintended mechanics is strictly prohibited.

5. No Griefing

  • Griefing is prohibited before, during, and after a raid.
  • This includes unnecessary destruction, rendering a base unusable, intentional loot destruction, or blocking exits without reason.
  • What constitutes griefing is determined by the Admin team in each case.

6. Building and Rebuild Rules

  • Building is not allowed during an active raid (including placing storage, placing code locks).
  • Rebuilding is only allowed 30 minutes after the last door has been destroyed.
  • Tracking this time is the responsibility of the defending team.

7. Logging Rules

  • Logging out inside a base that is not your own is prohibited.
  • Logging out with loot during a raid is prohibited.
  • Logging out at an active raid location to avoid consequences is not allowed.

8. Base Ownership Restrictions

  • Placing a code lock on another player’s base is prohibited.
  • Taking over another player’s base is not allowed.
  • Taking control of another player’s flagpole is prohibited.
  • If a flagpole has fully decayed, the base is considered abandoned and may be dismantled.

9. Excessive Dismantling

  • Dismantling a base excessively for materials is prohibited.
  • If a clear entry path exists, it must be used.

10. Raid Recording

  • Video recording of all raids is mandatory.
  • Recordings must be provided upon Admin request.
  • Recordings must be kept for 7 days.

11. Boosting Rules

  • Boosting with objects is prohibited.
  • Player boosting is allowed with a maximum of 2 players.
  • Vehicle boosting is allowed.
  • Any boosting that enables unintended entry is strictly prohibited.

12. Breaching Charge Requirements

  • Tier 1: 2 handmade or 1 heavy
  • Tier 2: 4 handmade or 2 heavy
  • Tier 3: 6 handmade or 3 heavy

13. Illegal Bases

Raiding bases marked with an "Illegal Base" sign is done at your own risk.

14. Helicopter Usage

  • Landing on or inside another player's base with a helicopter is prohibited.
  • Entering a base using a helicopter (including jumping from a hover) is not allowed.
  • Using helicopters to bypass raid rules is strictly prohibited.
  • Helicopters may not be used to access rooftops, enter bases, or initiate raids.

Admin Authority

Admins reserve the right to classify any raid or behavior as a rule violation if it involves exploits, provides an unfair advantage, or violates the spirit of the rules.
