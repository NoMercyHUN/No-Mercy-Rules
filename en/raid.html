Raid Rules

All raids must be conducted fairly. Any method intended to bypass the raid system or gain an unintended advantage is prohibited.

1. Raid Definition

A raid is defined as any intrusion into another player's base with the purpose of obtaining loot or opening the base, whether through a soft raid or a normal raid.

2. Raid Recording

Raiders must record the entire raid from the moment they enter the base until they leave it.

Raid recordings will not be visible to other players. Recordings may only be requested by the Admin Team.

Failure to record a raid will result in a 3-day ban.

Recordings must be kept for 5 days.

3. Normal Raid

Breaching Charges may only be used on doors, hangar doors, hatches, garage doors and gates.

Walls and windows may not be destroyed with explosives.

A raid is considered active for 60 minutes after the first C4 placement or the first wall dismantling.

The 60-minute timer resets whenever another door or gate is breached.

RAID TIMES

  • Friday: 18:00 – 24:00 (CEST)
  • Saturday: 16:00 – 24:00

SOFT RAID TIMES

24/7 – (Rules below)

4. Raid Tools

  • C4 – For breaching doors and gates
  • Hacking Tablet – Used to hack secured storage.
  • Hatchet – For dismantling walls

5. Rebuilding Rules

During a raid, only previously destroyed gates may be rebuilt and locked again.

New gates may not be built.

Defenders may build a new door if:

  • No damage has been dealt within the base for at least 60 minutes.
  • All attacking players have left the base area.

It is the defending team's responsibility to keep track of the timer.

6. Additional Rules for Defenders

6.1

Logging out inside a base with valuable items during an active raid is prohibited.

Admins may verify and investigate suspected violations.

If this rule is violated, the character may be killed and the items transferred to the raiding team.

This rule also applies if the raid continues through a server restart.

To help enforce this rule, the server uses a custom system. When logging out with certain valuable items, a Logout Crate may be automatically created at the location where the player disconnected.

The crate will contain the protected items and can be found and looted by any player.

The purpose of this system is to prevent players from avoiding raids or protecting valuable loot by logging out.

The Logout Crate system does not exempt players from punishment. The administration reserves the right to take further action against anyone found violating this rule.

6.2

Intentionally destroying your own loot during a raid is prohibited.

Examples include:

  • Dismantling storage containers
  • Shooting items until they become ruined

If you cannot defend your base, the loot no longer belongs to you.

Do not intentionally destroy loot.

Players violating this rule will receive a 48-hour ban and their base may be removed, requiring them to rebuild from scratch.

6.3

Blocking doors or forcing attackers into animations is prohibited.

6.4

Changing teams or swapping team members during raid hours is prohibited.

7. Additional Rules for Attackers

7.1. Logging out inside a base that is not your own is prohibited.

7.2. Logging out with raid loot is prohibited.

7.3. Logging out at an active raid location to avoid consequences is prohibited.

Example: Logging out while inside Pox Gas so the server moves you outside the enemy base after reconnecting.

7.4. Placing codelocks on another player's base is prohibited.

7.5. Taking ownership of another player's base or flagpole is prohibited.

7.6. Changing teams or team members during raid hours is prohibited.

7.7. Dismantling a base unnecessarily for building materials is prohibited. If there is a clear path into the base, that path must be used.

7.8. Blocking doors is prohibited.

7.9. Destroying acquired loot is prohibited.

Only take what you need.

Loot left behind may not be dumped on the ground and must be returned to storage containers.

8. Breaching Charge Guide

  • Tier 1: 2 Homemade BC or 1 Heavy BC
  • Tier 2: 4 Homemade BC or 2 Heavy BC
  • Tier 3: 6 Homemade BC or 3 Heavy BC

9. Soft Raid & Boosting Rules

9.1. Boosting using another player or one vehicle is allowed.

9.2. Stacking objects or multiple vehicles is prohibited.

9.3. Maximum boost limit: 1 player + 1 vehicle.

9.4. Helicopter boosting is PROHIBITED.

9.5. Entering a base by exploiting glitches or bugs is prohibited.

9.6. Exception: Climbing through vanilla windows without hitboxes or using vanilla ladders is not considered glitching.

All soft raids and boosting activities must be recorded.

Failure to do so may result in a ban.

10. Helicopter Usage

10.1. Landing a helicopter on or inside another player's base is prohibited.

  • If you land a helicopter inside an enemy base territory and exit the vehicle, the game will automatically move players outside the territory.
  • The helicopter will remain inside the territory and may be lost permanently.

We are unable to compensate helicopter losses resulting from this mechanic.
