Safe Zone Rules

The purpose of the Safe Zone is to provide a secure area for trading and player interaction.

1. PvP & Damage

  • Any form of PvP is prohibited inside the Safe Zone.
  • Killing, injuring, or knocking out other players is prohibited.
  • Running over players with vehicles is prohibited.
  • The use of explosives is prohibited.
  • Threatening players with weapons is prohibited.

2. Theft

  • Stealing items from other players is prohibited.
  • Stealing vehicles from other players is prohibited.
  • Taking items intentionally dropped by another player is prohibited.
  • Looting another player's corpse inside the Safe Zone is prohibited.

3. Vehicles

  • A vehicle parked inside the Safe Zone remains the property of the player who brought it there.
  • Entering another player's vehicle without permission is prohibited.
  • Blocking trader entrances, roads, or parking areas with vehicles is prohibited.
  • Intentionally disrupting traffic is prohibited.

4. Trader Usage

  • Traders must be used as intended.
  • Blocking or preventing other players from trading is prohibited.
  • Gathering around trader NPCs in a way that prevents access is prohibited.
  • Exploiting bugs or Safe Zone mechanics is prohibited.

5. Behavior

  • Harassing other players is prohibited.
  • Provoking conflicts inside the Safe Zone is prohibited.
  • Excessive noise or disruptive behavior is prohibited.
  • Following players around with the intention of annoying or disrupting them is prohibited.

6. Combat Logging & Escaping

  • Using the Safe Zone to escape an active PvP situation is prohibited.
  • The Safe Zone may not be used as a combat refuge.
  • Logging out immediately after PvP or while being chased is prohibited.

7. Ground Loot

  • Items dropped on the ground are left at the owner's own risk.
  • The Admin Team is not responsible for despawned or lost items.
  • Dropping excessive amounts of items and creating clutter is prohibited.

8. Safe Zone Abuse

  • Abusing Safe Zone protection for personal advantage is prohibited.
  • Playing around the Safe Zone border to gain an advantage is prohibited.
  • Luring players to the Safe Zone boundary for PvP purposes is prohibited.
  • Any attempt to abuse Safe Zone mechanics is prohibited.

9. Aircraft & Helicopters

  • Landing on players or vehicles is prohibited.
  • Dangerous flying around traders is prohibited.
  • Using aircraft to intentionally create chaos inside the Safe Zone is prohibited.

10. Admin Actions

  • The Admin Team may remove vehicles parked in prohibited areas.
  • The Admin Team may remove items placed in violation of the rules.
  • Violations of Safe Zone rules may result in warnings, temporary bans, or permanent bans.
