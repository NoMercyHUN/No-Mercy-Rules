Safe Zone – Trader Rules

1. PvP

1.1. You may not follow a player out of the safe zone with the intention of killing them later.

1.2. You may not wait around the trader area with the intention of killing players travelling there. (If you happen to meet and engage in PvP on the way, this rule does not apply.)

1.3. You may not use the safe zone as an escape from combat. If another player attacks you on the way, you must either defeat them or avoid them before entering the safe zone.

2. Safe Zone Protection Abuse

You may not abuse the invulnerability (god mode) provided by the safe zone.

Examples include:

2.1. Standing at the edge of the safe zone and repeatedly stepping in and out while shooting at players outside.

2.2. Flying a helicopter above the safe zone so players can jump out without taking damage.

3. Excessive Noise

Excessive noise inside the safe zone is prohibited.

Examples include:

  • Playing loud music through a microphone
  • Hovering above the safe zone with a helicopter for an extended period
  • Using megaphones
  • Any other disruptive noise-making behavior

4. Toxic Behaviour

TOXIC OR DISRUPTIVE BEHAVIOUR TOWARDS OTHER PLAYERS IS STRICTLY PROHIBITED.

5. Accessibility

Players may not intentionally block access to the trader or interfere with others attempting to use it.

6. Vehicles

Stealing vehicles from the safe zone is prohibited. A vehicle belongs to the player who drove it into the safe zone, regardless of whether another player possesses the key.

7. Theft

7.1. Taking items from another player's vehicle inventory or storage while inside the safe zone is prohibited.

7.2. Approaching another player's vehicle while they are managing inventory with the intention of "seeing if you can steal something" is prohibited. (This is considered toxic behaviour.)

Removing any item from a vehicle that does not belong to you may result in punishment or a ban, even if the item is immediately returned. It is not yours, do not touch it.

To prove theft within the safe zone, players must provide video evidence.

8. Ground Items

If an item falls onto the ground, anyone may pick it up. Players are responsible for ensuring they do not drop items they wish to keep and should always check that they have sufficient inventory space before using traders to prevent items from being automatically dropped.

No compensation will be provided for items lost in this way.

WARNING: Items left on the ground are automatically deleted after 3 hours.

9. Leaving Vehicles at the Trader

It is prohibited to leave a vehicle at the trader with the intention of later using it to attend a nearby event (Drop, KOTH, etc.). In such cases, vehicles must be removed from the safe zone.

WARNING: All vehicles inside the trader area are automatically deleted after 1 hour (counted from the moment the driver exits the vehicle).

If you plan to spend more time at the trader, you must re-enter the vehicle to reset the timer.

We are unable to compensate any vehicle losses resulting from this system.
